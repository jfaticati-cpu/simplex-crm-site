Simplex CRM privacy policy

Faticati · Updated October 8, 2026

Information we process

We store your account email, profile and business details, and client information, notes, campaign drafts and activity you enter. Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Records are separated by business.

Purchases

Apple processes iOS payments. RevenueCat receives your CRM account identifier and transaction information to verify annual or lifetime access; it does not receive your client records. We do not receive payment card details.

Optional iCloud backups

Backups you choose to create are stored in your private iCloud database under your Apple account. Client backups exclude passwords and service credentials.

External apps

Call, email, WhatsApp, SMS and maps actions open external apps or services. You control sending; those services have their own policies. Campaigns are drafts; automatic campaign sending is not enabled.

Use and sharing

We use information to provide the CRM, authenticate accounts, verify access and respond to support requests. We do not sell client data or use it for advertising tracking.

Retention and deletion

CRM records remain while your account is active or until deleted. Settings > Delete account removes your account, owned businesses and their CRM records. You may also remove private iCloud backups. Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or remove transaction records Apple retains. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, email simplecrm@faticati.com. This policy will be updated when new providers or features change these practices.

Optional ChatGPT connection

You may prepare contact information in your own ChatGPT account and paste the resulting JSON into Simplex CRM for review. If you authorize the optional direct connection, ChatGPT may send contact drafts you provide to your Simplex account. The connection cannot read your existing contact list or save clients directly. You review and confirm drafts in Simplex before saving. Disconnect in Settings to stop new drafts. Your use of ChatGPT is governed by your plan, usage limits and its policies. Simplex does not call a paid model API in this workflow.
