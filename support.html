Get help

Include your app version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or private client information.

Purchases

Free access supports up to 10 current clients. Annual and lifetime plans unlock unlimited clients. Restore purchases using the same CRM account that originally bought the plan. Apple confirms the local price before purchase.

Account deletion

Open Settings > Delete account and confirm with your password. You may choose to remove your private iCloud backups. Deleting your CRM account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Language

The app starts in English. Select Español in Settings to use Spanish.
